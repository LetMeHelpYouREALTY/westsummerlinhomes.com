Nearby Amenities in West Summerlin, Las Vegas

Published September 27, 2026

Golf, parks, Downtown Summerlin shopping, healthcare, and schools — what buyers ask about before they write an offer in Summerlin West.

Dining & Cafes

Downtown Summerlin (1980 Festival Plaza Dr) is the main dining destination for West Summerlin residents — national restaurant brands and local favorites in an open-air setting. Many villages are also a short drive to eateries along Charleston Blvd and Rampart Blvd.

Parks & Recreation

Exploration Peak Park (9700 S Buffalo Dr, Clark County) offers trails and views above the valley. Summerlin Centre Community Park (10588 Marketwalk Pl) serves newer Summerlin Centre villages. Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area is west of Summerlin for hiking and scenic drives.

Golf

Championship and public courses surround Summerlin West: Red Rock Country Club (2250-A Red Springs Dr), Angel Park Golf Club (100 S Rampart Blvd), and TPC Las Vegas (9851 Canyon Run Dr) are well-known options for residents and guests.

Healthcare

Summerlin Hospital Medical Center (657 N Town Center Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89144) is the primary hospital serving Summerlin. Urgent care and physician offices are also clustered near Downtown Summerlin and along Town Center Drive.

Shopping & Grocery

Downtown Summerlin combines department stores, specialty retail, and services. Whole Foods Market (2475 S Town Center Dr) sits in the district; Smith's Food and Drug (9851 W Charleston Blvd) is another frequent grocery stop serving the west valley.

Schools

Clark County School District serves Summerlin West. Buyers often research schools such as Palo Verde High School (333 S Pavilion Center Dr). Which CCSD schools are assigned to a West Summerlin address? Verify with the CCSD Zoning Search for that street address before you write an offer.

Commute & Key Destinations

  • Downtown Summerlin — in-area for many West Summerlin villages (minutes by car).
  • Las Vegas Strip — approximately 15–25 minutes by car depending on traffic and route (approximate).
  • Harry Reid International Airport — approximately 20–30 minutes by car (approximate).
  • Red Rock Canyon — typically under 30 minutes west from Summerlin West (approximate).
