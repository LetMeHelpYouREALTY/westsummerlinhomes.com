About Summerlin West

Summerlin West is the newest part of the Summerlin master-planned community on the western edge of the Las Vegas Valley. Neighborhoods sit close to Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area, with trails, parks, and mountain views throughout. Dr. Jan Duffy provides private driving tours and current MLS listings across Summerlin West.
